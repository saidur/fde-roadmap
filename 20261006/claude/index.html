What we do

From signed contract to working in production.

Software rarely fails because of the code alone. It fails at the edges: messy data, legacy systems, unclear workflows. That is where we work.

Software deployment

We install, configure, and tune platforms in your environment, whether cloud, on-premise, or air-gapped, and make sure teams can use them from day one.

Systems integration

We connect ERPs, CRMs, databases, devices, and internal tools with reliable pipelines, APIs, and adapters built around how your operation really runs.

Operational problem solving

We sit with operators, find the real bottleneck, and build the fix: scripts, workflows, dashboards, or product changes fed back to your engineering team.

Data migration & cleanup

We move data out of legacy systems safely, validating and reconciling as we go, so you can trust the numbers on the other side.

Training & enablement

We teach your people to run and extend what we build, with runbooks, walkthroughs, and office hours that leave capability behind.

Monitoring & support

After go-live we stay close: watching health, resolving incidents quickly, and tightening the system as real usage reveals new needs.

Why forward deployed

Closer to the problem means faster to the fix.

A ticket queue can't see your warehouse floor, your data quirks, or your approval chains. An embedded engineer can.

Remote-only support

  • Context lost between tickets and handoffs
  • Workarounds instead of root causes
  • Integrations designed from documentation alone
  • Users adopt slowly, or not at all

Fieldline embedded engineer

  • Direct access to operators and decision makers
  • Problems diagnosed in the real environment
  • Integrations built and tested against live systems
  • Adoption driven through hands-on enablement
Our approach

A simple, transparent engagement loop.

Every deployment follows four phases, with clear checkpoints so you always know where things stand.

Discover

We spend time on site mapping systems, workflows, stakeholders, and constraints, then agree on measurable goals.

Deploy

We stand up the software in your environment and connect it to the systems it depends on.

Iterate

We work side by side with users, shipping small fixes daily and resolving blockers as they appear.

Hand over

We document, train, and transition ownership, or stay on for ongoing support. Your choice.

FAQ

Questions teams ask before starting.

What is a Forward Deployed Engineer?

A software engineer embedded with a customer to deploy software, integrate systems, and solve operational problems directly in the customer's environment, rather than from a distance.

Do your engineers always work on site?

Our default is on site, because that is where the context is. We can structure a hybrid arrangement when travel, security, or logistics call for it.

Can you work with our legacy systems?

Yes. Much of our work involves connecting modern software to older systems, databases, and equipment that cannot easily be replaced.

How do you handle security and access?

We work within your policies: least-privilege access, your approved tooling, and your review processes. Specific requirements are agreed during scoping.

What happens when the engagement ends?

You receive documentation, runbooks, and trained staff. If you want continued coverage, we can move to an ongoing support arrangement.
