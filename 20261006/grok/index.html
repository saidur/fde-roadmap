Forward deployed engineering

An engineer on site until the system runs.

Fieldline embeds a forward deployed engineer with your team. They deploy the software into your environment, integrate the systems around it, and solve the operational problems that appear on site.

The role

A forward deployed engineer is an engineer embedded with your team to deploy software, integrate systems, and solve operational problems on site.

01 — The work

Deploy. Integrate. Solve it where it runs.

The engineer works in your environment, with the people who have to use what gets deployed. The stay lasts through the problems the site reveals.

  1. 01

    Deploy

    Stand the software up in the environment where it has to run: access, configuration, and a first use that follows the real workflow.

  2. 02

    Integrate

    Connect it to the systems the operation already depends on, including identity, records, events, and the tools beside it.

  3. 03

    Solve

    Work the failures that show up on site. A field that does not match, a step the workflow will not skip, a permission that stops the work.

  4. 04

    Hand back

    Leave the people who own the operation able to run it. The system is live, the runbook is with them, and the remaining work is named.

02 — Method

From a named site to a running system.

Each engagement has one site, one outcome, and an engineer present for the work between those two points.

  1. 01

    Brief

    Name the site, the outcome, and the systems on the path.

  2. 02

    Embed

    The engineer joins your team on site and works with the people who own the workflow.

  3. 03

    Land

    Deploy, integrate, and solve what the live operation reveals.

  4. 04

    Hand back

    Your team runs the system, with the notes and the access model to keep it running.

03 — Engagements

The shape follows the site.

Tell us what has to be true when the engineer leaves. The length of the stay follows that outcome.

01

Site embed

One site and one operational outcome, for as long as that outcome takes to run without the engineer in the loop.

02

Rollout

A first site sets the pattern. Later sites follow it, with an engineer present where that pattern meets a new site.

03

Resident

An engineer remains with your team while the deployment continues and the operation keeps changing.

What we need from you

The embed depends on access and on the people who can say yes at the site.

  • A named outcome for the site
  • Access to the environment
  • A counterpart who can make decisions there
  • Time with the people who do the work

04 — Questions

Before the brief.

What is a forward deployed engineer?

An engineer embedded with your team to deploy software, integrate systems, and solve operational problems on site.

Where does the work happen?

On site, in the environment the operation actually uses, with the people who use it.

Does the engineer change the software?

When the gap is in configuration or integration, they close it there. When the gap is in the software itself, they change the software so the site can run.

What do we need to provide?

A named outcome, access to the environment, someone who can make decisions at the site, and time with the people who do the work.

How does an engagement end?

When the agreed outcome is running on site, your team can operate it, and any remaining work is written down.

How do we begin?

Send a short brief. Include the site, the outcome, the systems on the path, and when it needs to be live.

05 — Brief
